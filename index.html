Heritage Stewardship Meets Precision Agronomy

Generational Farming in Gadhbar, Bah (Agra)

Located in Gadhbar, Jaitpur Kalan, Bah (Agra - 283114), Rajawat Farm House stands as a landmark of modern agricultural enterprise and generational dedication to pure soil health. Managed under the vision of Er. Prashant Rajawat, the farm implements scientific crop cycles, balanced rotary tillage, and clean irrigation.

Whether supplying bulk grain lots of Sharbati wheat, premium unpolished pulses (Moong and Urad dal), nutritious pearl millet (Bajra), or fresh Agra potato stock, we eliminate middlemen so buyers receive genuine farmgate produce at current mandi prices.

Modern Mechanization

Rotary tillers and laser levelers ensuring optimum crop seedbeds and moisture retention.

Pure Water Irrigation

Clean underground water ditches nurturing healthy crop roots across the entire acreage.
